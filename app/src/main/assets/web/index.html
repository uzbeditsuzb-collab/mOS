mOS 1.0 launcher

Interactive prototype for Samsung Galaxy A06 · 720 × 1600 px (360 × 800 dp)

Try it

  • Swipe upApp drawer with search and favourites
  • Swipe downControl Center
  • Long-press HomeWallpaper, new folder, settings
  • Long-press an appAdd to Home, favourite, app info
  • Tap a folderRename it, add or remove apps
  • MouseDrag, or scroll the wheel on Home. Esc is Back.
