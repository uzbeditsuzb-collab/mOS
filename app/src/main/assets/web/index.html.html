mOS 1.0 launcher

Interactive prototype for Samsung Galaxy A06 · 720 × 1600 px (360 × 800 dp)

Try it

  • Swipe up / downApp drawer and Control Center follow your finger
  • Swipe sidewaysHome pages, shown as dots above the dock
  • Hold an app, moveReorder; hold over an app for a folder; drop on a folder, the dock, a screen edge for the next page, or Remove at the top
  • Hold and releaseApp or folder menu (Shift+F10 on a keyboard)
  • Long-press HomeWallpaper, new folder, add page, settings
  • Tap a folderRename it, add apps, reorder or drag them out
  • MouseDrag; wheel up or down for drawer and Control Center, sideways for pages. Esc is Back.
